## The classes

Six fighters and two kings. Each fighter starts with a melee weapon and a signature
ranged or magic weapon.

| Class | Melee | Signature weapon |
|---|---|---|
| **Archer** | Spear | Bow |
| **Druid** | Scimitar | Staff |
| **Heretic** | Hammer | Claw Rod |
| **Paladin** | Longsword | Crossbow |
| **Sorceress** | Dagger | Wand |
| **Warrior** | Berserker Axe | Throwing Axes |
| **Good King** / **Evil King** | No weapons. Protect them (or hunt them). | |

## The loadout

Every fighter has four weapon slots:

1. **Advanced:** an upgrade bought or found mid-match: a better bow, a stronger staff,
   a wand that hits harder.
2. **Basic:** your class's starting ranged or magic weapon.
3. **Melee:** always with you.
4. **Utility:** scrolls and single-use items: haste, teleport, and a few surprises.

Upgrades come from the **buy menu**.

## The weapons

43 weapons, each with its own feel, taken from the original game's data:

- **Crossbows** that fire a spread of three bolts.
- **Throwing axes and hammers** that tumble through the air and bounce off walls.
- **Staves** that launch fireballs or leave lingering poison clouds.
- **Wands** that fire instant beams. One knocks enemies across the room.
- **Mines, grenades and scrolls** for the creative.

Projectiles leave scorch marks that match what they hit (stone, wood, metal, dirt), and
explosions hurt anyone nearby, sometimes you.

## Views

Play in **first person** like the original, or flip to **third person** at any time.
