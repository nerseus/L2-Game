## Voice commands

A **team commands** menu lists your character's own voice lines on keys 1 to 9, 0, - and =.
Press one and your teammates see the text and hear the line in your character's voice.

## Chat

- **Chat** to everyone, and **team chat**.
- A typing mode that locks the game keys while you type.
- New input actions that players can rebind.
