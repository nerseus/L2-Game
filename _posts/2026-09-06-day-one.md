Some games don't get a second life. Twenty-odd years ago there was an FPS where you
didn't pick a rifle, you picked a *class*: a Paladin with a longsword and crossbow, a
Druid with a scimitar and a staff of green fire, a Sorceress with a dagger and a wand.
It had a small, fierce community. Then it was abandoned, and it disappeared.

This diary follows the attempt to rebuild it.
<!--more-->

## Why rebuild it?

Because nothing has replaced it. Plenty of games have swords and plenty have magic, but
very few drop you into a fast, competitive arena where a crossbow bolt, a thrown axe
and a fireball all exist in the same fight. The original's mix of medieval weapons and
spellcasting is still unusual.

## The foundation

L2 is built on **Unity 6** with **Photon Fusion** for networking. Fusion gives
server-authoritative multiplayer with client-side prediction, so hits feel instant
without letting anyone cheat their way to them. I started from a working multiplayer
shooter template and began replacing everything that made it a modern military game.

## The first week

- **Day 1:** engine, networking and an empty test arena.
- **Day 2:** the first original character model imported and standing in the scene.
- **Day 3:** characters wired up as real, networked, playable fighters: walking,
  crouching, sprinting.
- **Day 4:** character select and the original main menu art in place.

It's rough, but by the end of the first week a Paladin could walk around a test map
while another player watched.
