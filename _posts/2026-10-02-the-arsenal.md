Every weapon from the original game is now in L2: all 43, from the humble dagger to
crossbows that fire three bolts at once. Here's a tour of what went in.
<!--more-->

## Four slots

Each fighter carries up to four weapons:

1. **Advanced:** an upgrade, bought mid-match.
2. **Basic:** your class's own ranged or magic weapon.
3. **Melee:** always there when things get close.
4. **Utility:** scrolls and single-use tricks.

The new **buy menu** is where upgrades come from. A Paladin who saves up can trade the
standard crossbow for something nastier.

## Straight from the source

Damage, attack timing, recovery, projectile speed and range all come from the original
game's own data tables rather than being re-tuned by feel. A scimitar swing lands
exactly 0.2 seconds after you click, just like it did twenty years ago.

## Things that fly

Projectiles got a lot of attention:

- **Arcs and bounces.** Throwing axes and hammers tumble end over end and bounce off
  walls, losing height with each bounce.
- **Splash.** Explosions hurt everyone nearby, including you if you're careless, and
  kick your aim up when they hit.
- **Knockback.** The Wand of Force throws people across the room.
- **Mines and grenades** that settle and wait.

## Leaving a mark

Every impact leaves the right scorch mark for the surface it hit: stone, wood, metal,
glass, dirt or cloth. The old engine stored a surface type on every wall texture, and
L2 reads it back the same way.
