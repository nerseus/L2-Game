Each map has an objective, and the Legends Standard mode plays it. Two are now in.

## Warlord Escape

One player is the **Warlord**. The round has its own win conditions, messages and sounds:
the Warlord escaping, or being killed, decides it, with gold for the killer and the Warlord's
team.

## Sword in the Stone

A sword sits in a stone. Draw it and carry it to a rescue zone. The sword goes home if its
bearer dies. Drawing it, delivering it and killing the bearer all pay gold.

## Rules

- Rounds are won by wiping out the other team. A round that runs out of time is decided by
  the objective.
- Sword in the Stone and the dragon mode are draws on time.

Also in this change: monster research notes, rescue zones and a general cleanup.
