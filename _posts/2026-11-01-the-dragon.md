The last big actor and game mode.

- **Dragon AI.** It sleeps until someone hits it, then fights with its claws, wings and
  breath.
- **Fire.** Breath and fire bolts were tuned.
- **Winces.** It winces at 75%, 50% and 25% health, which pays a bonus to whoever's hit takes
  it there.
- **Slay the Dragon.** The team of the player whose hit kills the Dragon wins.
- **Gold.** Waking it pays 500, killing it 1,500 plus a team share.
- More navigation mesh cleanup.
