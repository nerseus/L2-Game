Armor is in.

- **The buy menu** sells armor sets, and each one changes the character's skin.
- **Damage reduction.** Armor reduces damage by the **hit location** (head, chest, arms,
  legs), using the original's damage table.
- **Weight.** Heavier armor slows your run.
- **Disabled weapon icon.** A weapon you can't use shows a disabled icon on the HUD.
- **Debug panel.** Movement speed now shows in the debug panel, handy for checking the armor
  slowdown.
