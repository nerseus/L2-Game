The maps have monsters now: orcs, bandits, liches, spiders, wights, troglodytes, dwarves, and
more.

## How they work

The original ran small scripts on top of its engine. L2 hard-codes the behaviours in C#, one
per family, with the original's data tables supplying the numbers: health, attacks, speeds,
reach, wince thresholds.

- **Navigation meshes** are built for each map and used for pathing.
- **Basic behaviour:** see a player, run at them, attack in range. Ranged monsters throw or
  shoot on a timer, with the right projectiles.
- **Attacks fire from animation frames**, like the original, so the clip sets the timing.
- **Sounds** for each monster.
- **Destructibles** such as the dagrell and props.
- **Debris** and gibs when things break.
